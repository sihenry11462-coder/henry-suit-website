The Process

How Made-to-Measure Works

No shop visits required. We bring the perfect fit to your doorstep.

01

Choose & Measure

Select your suit. If you know your exact sizes (Height, Waist, Coat), you provide them. If you only know your waist size, we will recommend the best fit for you using our chart below.

02

The Tailoring

We take your measurements (or your recommended size) and ensure the suit is perfectly adjusted to your body shape before it is finalized.

03

Home or Office Delivery

Once perfectly tailored, your 100% Turkish wool suit is delivered directly to your home or workplace.
